# Stories

Every story repo under [domhan-an-fiosracht](https://github.com/domhan-an-fiosracht).

| Universe | Universe Repo | Tagline | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Porchlight | [porchlight](https://github.com/domhan-an-fiosracht/porchlight) | oh dear god | Cryptids lived quietly inside human cities until a zombie virus broke the masquerade at its ugliest — humanity's first look at them was infected werewolves rampaging on phone footage. Survivors on both sides rebuild with no map of who's dangerous, or who's doomed. |
| Safehouses | [safehouses](https://github.com/domhan-an-fiosracht/safehouses) | A Recounting of the Final Divide, and the Years It Took to Cross It | After centuries in hiding, cryptids reveal themselves in 2037 when dragons land at the world's capitals asking for peaceful integration. In the US, a coalition that has quietly run things from under Montana for centuries steps into public view to lead a joint peacekeeping force — through protests, war, and the safehouses after. |
| Shipfall | [shipfall](https://github.com/domhan-an-fiosracht/shipfall) | The Huntress, the Alien, and the Wolf | Three years after alien ships came down, the invasion has stalled into an occupation neither side can end: the Thaeli are cut off from home and grounded for good in the coastal Zones, while humanity regrows its towns, armies, and myths in everything they left behind. |
| Sryth | [sryth](https://github.com/domhan-an-fiosracht/sryth) | No Man's Sky shows up to Earth | Earth is dying of pollution when a portal opens in the Gulf of Mexico — the last of eight, built by seven other species, all leading to the same unspoiled planet. On Sryth, humanity joins a finished alien city late, under clean-only laws, a sun that barely moves, and a calendar that counts down to winter. |
| The Good Neighbors | [the-good-neighbors](https://github.com/domhan-an-fiosracht/the-good-neighbors) | She tripped over a werewolf on the way to first contact | Earth was never alone. A galactic network of hundreds of civilizations considers humanity's isolation an oversight, and dragons, werewolves, and Leviathans have been running hidden cities under the human map all along. First contact means meeting both. |
| Undercurrent | [undercurrent](https://github.com/domhan-an-fiosracht/undercurrent) | Story: Women save the world, again | Magic is a force of physics that anyone could learn, and cryptids are built around it. After a one-week ultimatum, the cryptids take over the US government "until the country is fixed" and order everyone back to normal life — while any neighbor, classmate, or official could be something else. |
